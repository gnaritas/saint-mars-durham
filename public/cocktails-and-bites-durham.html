The list

Short, sharp, and built to sit well with a record

The cocktail list trades the old tiki era for drinks that reward attention: a Black Manhattan, and a martini served the way it should be — with olives, almonds and giardiniera on the side. The full list lives at the bar; ask your bartender what the room is in the mood for.

From upstairs

Bites come down from Bar Virgile

Saint Mars is the listening room; Bar Virgile is the restaurant one floor up, owned by the same team. Bites come down from the kitchen while you stay for the record — order early, eat between sides.

You do not have to leave the room for anything. That is the design.
