Privacy policy

This site collects about as little as a website can. Here is the entire story.

What we collect

Only what you send us

The contact form on this site sends your name, and any phone number or email you choose to add, along with your message, straight to the bar's inbox so a person can answer it. Nothing is stored on this site itself — the pages are plain HTML with no accounts, no sign-ups, and no newsletters.

Email submitted through the form is used only to reply to your message. It is not added to any marketing list, and it is never sold.

Cookies & tracking

No cookies here. One caveat, downstairs neighbors aside.

This site sets no first-party cookies and runs no analytics, no pixels, and no scripts of any kind. The pages are static, so there is nothing to profile you with.

The directions map on this site is a Google Maps embed. When it loads, Google may set its own cookies under its own privacy policy — that is the only third party anywhere on these pages, and you can open the location with the plain directions link instead if you prefer.
